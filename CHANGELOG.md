## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- Rest the laser pointer on a target and a hexagon closes on it; the scan comes out of the hexagon, each element
  on its own thin line. Sweeping the pointer shows nothing.
- Every named target: its name above it over a long bar, and the distance under it (it follows the pointer), as
  the laser pointer's own screen writes them.
- Creatures: WEAK TO (green, left) and RESISTS (red, right) with the game's damage type icons and names, and your
  kill count under them. Only for creatures your Miner's Manual already shows.
- Resources (veins, gems, chunks, collectibles, brewing ingredients): the objective it counts for, with the
  objective's own text, icon and counter, and how much of it you own.
- Machines and other named things: the objective whose text names them.
- Mod Hub page: Enabled on top; DETAILED SETTINGS opens one switch per element (name, distance, weak to and
  resists, kill count, objective, owned amount) and hides while the mod is off.
